# 3. ENROLLED AGENT (EA)

## What is EA?

An Enrolled Agent (EA) is a federally authorized U.S. tax practitioner empowered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury.

The iLead EA presentation describes EA as the highest credential the IRS can award to an individual.

EA professionals can represent taxpayers before the IRS in matters including:
- Audits
- Collections
- Appeals

## EA Work

Possible EA work includes:
- U.S. tax preparation
- IRS representation
- Audit support
- Collections support
- Taxpayer representation
- Work involving liens, levies and payment plans

## EA Eligibility

The iLead presentation states:
- Minimum age: 18+
- No nationality restriction
- No prior specialized qualification required
- Basic/foundational accounting understanding is useful

Two paths are described:

### Path 1 — Special Enrollment Examination
Pass:
1. Part 1 — Individuals
2. Part 2 — Businesses
3. Part 3 — Representation, Practices & Procedures

### Path 2 — IRS Work Experience
The presentation describes a qualifying IRS work-experience route involving a minimum of five years of relevant IRS work.

## Who Can Apply?

The presentation lists:
- 10+2 / Intermediate candidates with basic commerce/accounting understanding
- Graduates
- Postgraduates
- Homemakers
- Retired employees
- Working professionals
- Finance professionals

Applicants must be 18+.

## EA Career Opportunities

The presentation lists possible career directions:
- Tax Consultant / Advisor
- IRS Representation Specialist
- U.S. Tax Manager / Director
- Freelance / Remote Tax Practitioner
- Bookkeeping & Accounting Services
- Independent tax practice

These are career possibilities, not guarantees.

# 4. FUNDAMENTAL PAYROLL CERTIFICATION (FPC)

## What is FPC?

FPC stands for Fundamental Payroll Certification.

The supplied iLead FPC/CPP content describes FPC as an entry-level certification offered by PayrollOrg (formerly the American Payroll Association) for individuals who are new to payroll.

It is designed for:
- Payroll beginners
- Support staff
- People entering payroll
- Professionals who want to demonstrate foundational payroll knowledge

The supplied material states that no prior payroll experience is required to take the FPC exam.

## FPC Eligibility

The supplied brochure states that FPC is open to people who want to demonstrate baseline payroll competency.

It lists:
- 10+2 candidates
- Graduates
- Postgraduates from any stream
- Entry-level payroll professionals
- Managers and supervisors involved in payroll management
- Sales professionals/consultants serving the payroll industry
- Systems analysts/engineers supporting payroll systems
- Payroll service provider client representatives
- Homemakers/home makers seeking additional income opportunities
- Finance professionals

The brochure states that PayrollOrg membership is not required to register for or take the FPC examination.

## FPC Exam

The supplied FPC/CPP content states:
- 150 MCQs including 25 pretest questions
- Passing score: 300 out of a maximum scaled score of 500
- Exam fee listed in the supplied content: $510
- Validity listed: 3 years
- Course code: FPC-60
- iLead course duration listed: 33 hours
- iLead classes listed: 21
- iLead modules listed: 10

IMPORTANT:
Exam fees and official exam rules can change. Do not present old figures as permanently current without confirmation.

## FPC Exam Mode

The supplied content states that the exam may be conducted:
- Online
- Offline at a test centre

Pearson VUE is identified as the testing provider in the supplied material.

# 5. CERTIFIED PAYROLL PROFESSIONAL (CPP)

## What is CPP?

CPP stands for Certified Payroll Professional.

The supplied iLead FPC/CPP content describes CPP as an advanced payroll certification offered by PayrollOrg.

It is intended for experienced payroll professionals and covers advanced areas such as:
- Payroll systems
- Taxation
- Payroll management
- Strategic payroll practices

## CPP Eligibility

The supplied material gives these eligibility routes:

A candidate may qualify through one of the following:
1. Minimum 3 years of payroll experience
   - India, U.S. or other payroll experience as stated in the supplied content
2. At least 24 months of PayrollOrg-approved training courses before the exam
3. Active FPC certification + 18 months of payroll experience

## CPP Exam

The supplied FPC/CPP content states:
- 190 MCQs including 25 pretest questions
- Passing score: 300 out of a maximum scaled score of 500
- Exam fee listed: $660
- Course code: CPP-60

# 6. FPC VS CPP — SIMPLE EXPLANATION

When a student asks the difference:

FPC is the entry-level payroll certification and is suitable for people starting or building foundational payroll knowledge.

CPP is the advanced payroll certification and is intended for candidates who meet the required experience/training eligibility.

Simple voice answer:

"FPC is generally the beginner-level payroll certification, so it's suitable if you're starting in payroll. CPP is more advanced and has experience or training eligibility requirements."

If the student's background is unclear, ask:
"Do you already have payroll experience? If yes, roughly how many years?"
