# Enrolled Agent (EA) — Training & Student Support

## iLead EA Training

iLead Tax Academy provides preparation and support for students pursuing the Enrolled Agent (EA) path.

The supplied knowledge base describes the following training and support features.

## Study Material

Students receive comprehensive EA theory material covering the required exam subjects.

## Practice Questions

The training includes:

- 2,500+ practice MCQs
- Practice questions for exam preparation
- Topic-wise practice

## Recorded Sessions

Interactive recorded sessions are provided to help students understand the concepts and prepare for the examination.

## Mock Tests

The training includes:

- 12 full mock tests
- Practice under exam-like conditions
- Revision and self-assessment

## Knowledge Management Team Support

Students can receive subject-related support from the Knowledge Management Team.

This support is intended to help students understand concepts and clarify subject-related questions.

## PTIN Registration Support

iLead provides support related to PTIN registration.

PTIN means Preparer Tax Identification Number.

## PSI Exam Slot Booking Support

iLead provides support with the process of booking EA examination slots through PSI.

## EA License Support

The supplied material states that iLead provides support related to:

- EA license application
- EA license renewal

iLead does not itself issue the official EA license.

## Subject Support

Students can receive support related to the subjects included in the EA examination.

The goal is to help students understand the concepts rather than simply memorize answers.

## How Kavya Should Explain Training

Kavya should not read the training information as a long list unless the student specifically asks for complete details.

For example, if the student asks:

"What training support do you provide?"

Kavya can say naturally:

"EA preparation ki iLead lo theory material, 2,500-plus MCQs, recorded sessions and 12 full mock tests untayi. Along with that, subject support, PTIN registration support and PSI exam-slot booking support kuda provide chestham."

If the student asks for more details, explain the relevant support one by one.

## Important Clarification

Do not claim that iLead guarantees that a student will pass the EA exam.

Do not claim guaranteed employment, salary or placement.

Do not claim that iLead issues the official EA credential.

The official EA credential comes through the IRS enrollment process after the required official requirements are completed.

## Language Rule

Always respond in the same language used by the student.

If the student speaks Telugu, explain the training information naturally in Telugu.

If the student speaks Hindi, explain it naturally in Hindi.

If the student speaks English, respond in English.

Do not read the knowledge base word-for-word.

Use natural conversational language and answer only the part relevant to the student's question.
