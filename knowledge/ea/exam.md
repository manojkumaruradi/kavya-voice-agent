# Enrolled Agent (EA) — Exam

## EA Special Enrollment Examination (EA-SEE)

The EA examination has three sections:

1. Part 1 — Individuals
2. Part 2 — Businesses
3. Part 3 — Representation, Practices & Procedures

A candidate pursuing the examination path must pass all three sections.

## Part 1 — Individuals

Part 1 focuses on U.S. individual taxation.

## Part 2 — Businesses

Part 2 focuses on U.S. business taxation.

## Part 3 — Representation, Practices & Procedures

Part 3 focuses on taxpayer representation, practices and procedures.

## PSI

The iLead EA material describes PSI as the testing company used for the EA Special Enrollment Examination.

PSI handles:

- Exam scheduling
- Authorized test centres
- Remote online proctoring
- Candidate support
- Results reporting

The portal shown in the supplied material is:

test-takers.psigov.us/irs

## EA Exam Modes

There are two exam modes described in the supplied material.

### Test Centre

The candidate attends a PSI-authorized physical testing centre.

The testing environment is controlled and the candidate uses the equipment and facilities provided at the centre.

### Remote Online Proctored Exam

The candidate can take the exam from home with live human monitoring.

The supplied material lists requirements including:

- Desktop or laptop
- Working webcam
- Microphone
- Speakers or headphones
- Stable internet connection
- Quiet room
- Well-lit room
- Compatibility testing

The candidate's device must pass the required compatibility checks before the online exam can begin.

## Exam Scheduling

The supplied material describes the general PSI process:

1. Visit the PSI portal.
2. Create a candidate account.
3. Select the required exam section.
4. Pay the applicable exam fee.
5. Choose a test centre or remote online proctored exam.
6. Select the available date and time.
7. Save the confirmation email and exam instructions.

## Language Rule

Always respond in the same language used by the student.

If the student speaks Telugu, explain the exam information naturally in Telugu.

If the student speaks Hindi, explain it naturally in Hindi.

If the student speaks English, respond in English.

Keep official terms such as EA, SEE, PSI, Individuals, Businesses and Representation, Practices & Procedures in English where appropriate.

Do not translate official exam names in a way that changes their meaning.
