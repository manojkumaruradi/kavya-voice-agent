# Enrolled Agent (EA) — Overview

## What is an Enrolled Agent?

An Enrolled Agent (EA) is a federally authorized U.S. tax practitioner empowered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury.

The iLead EA presentation describes EA as the highest credential the IRS can award to an individual.

EA professionals can represent taxpayers before the IRS in matters including:

- Audits
- Collections
- Appeals

## What Does an EA Do?

Possible EA work includes:

- U.S. tax preparation
- IRS representation
- Audit support
- Collections support
- Taxpayer representation
- Work involving liens, levies and payment plans

## EA Career Directions

Possible career directions include:

- Tax Consultant / Advisor
- IRS Representation Specialist
- U.S. Tax Manager / Director
- Freelance / Remote Tax Practitioner
- Bookkeeping & Accounting Services
- Independent tax practice

These are career possibilities and are not guarantees of employment.

## Important Conversation Rule

When explaining EA information to a student:

- Do not read this document word-for-word.
- Explain the information naturally and conversationally.
- Adapt the explanation to the student's question.
- Keep the answer concise unless the student asks for more detail.
