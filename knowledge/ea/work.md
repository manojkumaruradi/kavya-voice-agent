# Enrolled Agent (EA) — Work & Responsibilities

## What Work Does an Enrolled Agent Do?

An Enrolled Agent can work in areas related to U.S. taxation and taxpayer representation.

Possible areas of work include:

- U.S. tax preparation
- IRS representation
- Audit support
- Collections support
- Taxpayer representation
- Support related to liens
- Support related to levies
- Payment-plan related work

## IRS Representation

One important area of EA work is representing taxpayers before the IRS.

This can involve matters such as:

- Audits
- Collections
- Appeals

## Tax Preparation

An EA can work on U.S. tax-related services and tax preparation.

The exact responsibilities can vary depending on the person's role, experience, employer and professional setup.

## Career Roles Related to EA

EA-related career directions can include:

- Tax Consultant / Advisor
- IRS Representation Specialist
- U.S. Tax Manager / Director
- Freelance / Remote Tax Practitioner
- Bookkeeping & Accounting Services
- Independent tax practice
