# Enrolled Agent (EA) — Eligibility

## Who Can Become an Enrolled Agent?

The iLead EA program states that there is no specialized qualification required to pursue the EA path.

A person can pursue the EA path without a specific accounting or tax qualification.

Basic accounting knowledge can be useful, but it is not stated as a mandatory qualification.

## Basic Eligibility

According to the iLead EA knowledge base:

- Minimum age: 18 years
- No nationality restriction is specified
- No specialized qualification is required
- Basic accounting knowledge is useful

## Two Main Paths to Become an EA

There are two main paths described for becoming an Enrolled Agent.

### Path 1 — Special Enrollment Examination (SEE)

A person can qualify through the IRS Special Enrollment Examination.

The examination has three parts:

1. Individuals
2. Businesses
3. Representation, Practices & Procedures

After completing the required examination and official IRS requirements, the person can proceed through the IRS enrollment process.

### Path 2 — IRS Work Experience

The knowledge base also describes an alternative path based on qualifying experience with the IRS.

This path is for individuals who have qualifying experience as an IRS employee in a position involving interpretation and application of U.S. tax laws.

## Important Clarification

EA eligibility should not be explained as requiring:

- CA qualification
- U.S. CPA qualification
- B.Com qualification
- A specific accounting degree

The iLead EA knowledge base does not state these as mandatory requirements for pursuing the EA path.

Kavya should answer based on the student's situation.

For example:

If a student asks:

"Can a B.Com graduate do EA?"

Do not simply read the knowledge base.

Explain naturally:

"Yes, you can pursue EA. A specific accounting qualification is not mandatory. Your B.Com background can be useful because basic accounting knowledge helps, but you don't need CA or U.S. CPA to start the EA path."

If the student asks:

"I am from engineering background. Can I do EA?"

Kavya should explain that a specialized accounting qualification is not mandatory, while basic understanding of accounting and tax concepts can be useful.
