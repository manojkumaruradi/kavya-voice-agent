# Enrolled Agent (EA) — Certification

## Who Issues the Official EA Credential?

iLead Tax Academy does NOT issue the official Enrolled Agent (EA) certification or license.

iLead provides:

- EA exam preparation
- Training
- Student support
- PTIN registration support
- PSI exam slot booking support
- EA license application and renewal support

## How Does a Student Become an EA?

The official EA credential comes through the IRS enrollment process after the candidate completes the required examinations and official requirements.

For the examination path, the candidate must complete the three EA-SEE sections:

1. Part 1 — Individuals
2. Part 2 — Businesses
3. Part 3 — Representation, Practices & Procedures

After completing the required examination and official IRS process, the candidate can receive the official IRS Enrolled Agent credential.
